Doctor Appointment Booking System - Requirements Specification

Chosen N: 3 (Maximum upcoming appointments per patient at any given time)

Requirements:
R1 [Functional] A patient can view the days and times when a doctor is available.
R2 [Functional] A patient can book an appointment during an available time slot.
R3 [Functional] A doctor can accept or decline an appointment request.
R4 [Functional] A patient can have at most N upcoming appointments at any given time.
R5 [Non-functional] Patient medical data shall be accessible only to authorized staff.
R6 [Non-functional] Available appointment times shall be displayed within 2 seconds.
R7 [Non-functional] The application shall be usable on mobile phone screens.

Implementation Scope:
- R1-R4: Implemented in working application flow.
- For R4, N = 3. Future Pending and Accepted appointments count toward this limit. Declined, Cancelled, and past appointments do not count.
- R5-R7: Non-functional requirements explained conceptually in design and documentation.
